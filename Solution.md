# The solution is very simple, even without using any tools.

Behind the person was a store with a sign that read ***"Julien."*** The building has a rounded shape.
As mentioned earlier in the task, this is a neighborhood in Paris.

All I had to do was go to Google Maps, type in the name from the sign, 
check a couple of photos taken from different angles, and the building was found
From there, it’s even easier: either copy the address and search Google for - “where is the nearest metro station,” 
or search for it yourself on Google Maps.
